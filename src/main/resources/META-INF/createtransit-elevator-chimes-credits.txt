Create: Transit — six additional elevator chime presets

All six listed source assets are CC0-1.0.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

These files retain their CC0 dedication independently of the mod license.
Five use public Freesound HQ previews converted to mono Vorbis; the sixth
is an original OpenGameArt OGG converted to mono Vorbis. No manufacturer
endorsement is claimed. Some recordings include doors/background noise.
