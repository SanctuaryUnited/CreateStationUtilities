The model elevator door hiss and motor hum are original procedural audio, generated by tools/generate_elevator3_audio.py using deterministic noise and oscillators. No proprietary recordings were sampled. These two new assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0-1.0. Existing original elevator arrival cues are reused.


Dev3 adds six CC0 source assets listed in the JAR's META-INF/createtransit-elevator-chimes-cc0.json, with companion author credits. Their dedication applies independently of other mod assets. See ELEVATORS3_CHIMES_DEV3.md for the source-quality and brand-identification notes.
